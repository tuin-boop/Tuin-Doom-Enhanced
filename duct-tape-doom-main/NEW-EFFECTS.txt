Selected upstream effects — 19 October 2026

Integrated from the supplied derp-main.zip, retaining the local renderer,
lighting workers, FluidWeapons integration and AI improvements.

F5 > Effects > Combat:
- Hit flinches and adjustable reaction strength.
- Death reactions, varied deaths and procedural falls.
- Wound holes attached to the monster artwork as animation frames change.
These are presentation effects; they do not change monster hitboxes or damage.

F5 > Atmosphere:
- Torch embers: capped at 384 nearby particles.
- Shore ripples: capped at 2048 blocks and limited to nearby liquid edges.
- Glowing liquid edges using the existing liquid artwork.

F5 > Sound:
- Near-miss bullet sounds for distant enemy shots passing close to the player.

Each effect can be turned off individually. New glass was deliberately deferred.

Validation: 27 test cases passed, including real-WAD GPU gameplay/save-load,
weapon interpolation, wound attachment and placement, near-miss classification,
and shader compilation. Fullscreen visual captures checked at 2560x1440.
A short E1M1 realtime plasma run with effects enabled averaged 134.7 FPS,
p99 13.13 ms, maximum 39.84 ms; 7 frames exceeded 16.67 ms.
This is a limited smoke measurement, not a guarantee for complex maps.

Shore ripples are OFF by default, with [High cost] in the menu.
Selected effects now show approximate performance notes. Costs depend on
hardware and map complexity; baked-light options distinguish loading cost
from per-frame cost. The same short run with only shore ripples disabled
averaged 234.5 FPS (p99 10.35 ms).
