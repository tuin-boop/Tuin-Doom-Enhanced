# Tuin-Doom-Enhanced — coloured destruction, voxels and liquid effects

- Surface-coloured impacts and rubble, refined heat/plasma/BFG effects, and further destruction and moving-geometry fixes.
- Liquid-impact splashes for rockets, smaller projectiles and bullets; improved environmental options and dependent menu controls.
- Native VoxelDoom v2.4 support: add your separately downloaded pack in the launcher. F4 reports detection and provides model/eye-glow toggles. Models use environmental lighting; coplanar surface batching reduces geometry while preserving voxel colours.
- Improved Fluid rocket presentation, glass support breaking, and an optional PS2-style filter.
- Type `johnromerogod` in single player for sticky rockets that burst into 25 nonrecursive rockets. Right-click fires 25 drunken rockets: they first follow your aim, including pitch, then wander. The cheat triggers a shaking Doomguy popup with the Toasty sound.

## Download and use
Download **Tuin-Doom-Enhanced.exe**, run it, and select your own Doom-compatible IWAD. No commercial IWAD or VoxelDoom pack is included. Existing settings are preserved. Older releases remain available.

Voxel support loads native models, not the pack's ZScript. Sprite-specific deformation effects do not apply to voxel models. Performance varies with map complexity, damage and monster counts. Destruction can bypass intended map progression.

## Validation
Release builds, Doom 1/2 voxel decoding and rendering, launcher pack detection, menu checks, upward rocket aim, 25-projectile counts and nonrecursive child guards passed. Popup visuals were inspected and Toasty playback confirmed. These checks are not exhaustive custom-map testing.
