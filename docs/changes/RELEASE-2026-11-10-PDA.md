# Field PDA and compact discoveries

- Browse encountered monsters, weapons, items, powerups and objects in the Field PDA; includes Doomguy, fists and pistol entries.
- Open with P or the PDA button outside the weapon wheel on the right.
- Normal-colour previews, solid voxel models and mouse-drag rotation; Spectres retain a translucent, flickering shadow appearance.
- Custom smaller hand cursor, updated fist/pistol artwork and additional lore.
- Optional compact green discovery cards beside the right edge: rotating preview, name and short detail, with slide-in and fade-out. Discoveries queue without overlapping and remain off by default.
- Weapon pickup notices appear on the left, away from discovery cards.
- Normal exit-switch behaviour retained; the experimental exit portal and its setting are removed. Legacy experimental saves are handled on load.

Existing GPU voxel improvements, LAN multiplayer and other features remain included. Both multiplayer PCs should use this same build and matching game files. Commercial IWADs and VoxelDoom are not bundled.

Validation: Release build, discovery queue/timing tests and PDA render checks. Engine and mod credits are retained, including Duct-Tape DOOM. Older releases remain available.
